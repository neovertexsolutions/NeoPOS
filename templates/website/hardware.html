Hardware Compatibility & Diagnostics

Kelra connects seamlessly with industry-standard thermal printers, 1D/2D barcode scanners, automatic cash drawers, and touchscreen registers.

Thermal Printers

Full support for 80mm & 58mm thermal receipt printers via USB, LAN Ethernet, and Bluetooth.

Epson TM-T20/T88 Xprinter XP-58/80 Bixolon SRP Rongta ESC/POS

Barcode Scanners

Standard USB HID Keyboard wedge scanners. Instant recognition with automatic enter/newline keycode.

Honeywell Voyager Zebra DS2208 Eyoyo 2D QR Inateck 2.4G UPC/EAN/Code128

Auto Cash Drawers

Connects directly to the receipt printer via RJ11 / RJ12 kick-out cable. Opens automatically on invoice completion.

RJ11 / RJ12 24V M-S Cash Drawer Posiflex CR-4000 APG Vasario

Pro Tip: Enable 1-Click Silent Printing (No Print Dialog)

In high-volume supermarkets and busy retail checkout counters, eliminate the browser print popup by launching Chrome or Edge with silent kiosk printing flags:

chrome.exe --kiosk --kiosk-printing --app=http://127.0.0.1:8000/pos/
