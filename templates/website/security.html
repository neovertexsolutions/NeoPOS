Multi-Tier Defense Architecture

Enterprise Security for Retail POS

Protecting your checkout cash drawers, margins, employee permissions, and sales audit trails against tampering and loss.

Granular Staff RBAC

Strict separation between Admin, Manager, and Cashier accounts prevents cashiers from manipulating sales margins, voiding invoices unnoticed, or viewing wholesale purchase prices.

PBKDF2 Cryptographic Salt

Passwords are irreversibly hashed using PBKDF2 SHA-256 with individual cryptographic salt seeds. Even in database exports, employee passwords cannot be decrypted.

ACID Power-Loss Defense

All financial invoice creations and inventory subtractions utilize atomic database transactions. If store electricity cuts mid-sale, the database automatically rolls back clean without corruption.

Staff Role & Permission Boundaries

Clear operational boundaries prevent cashier shrink, inventory inflation, and unauthorized discounts.
